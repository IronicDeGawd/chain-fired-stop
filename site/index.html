My bot held the stop-loss and went offline. The chain sold by itself.

Same long, same stop, two ways. One stop is held by a bot that checks the price every 2 seconds. The other is held by a contract that Somnia itself wakes every 5 seconds. A few seconds in, I cut the bot's connection. When the price fell through the stop, the chain sold on its next check, with nobody signing. The bot's stop was down for 200 seconds.

23 Oct 2026 · Somnia Tokyo testnet · dreamDEX ETH perp · every transaction on the explorer · @0xironyAditya

Verdict. A stop-loss is only as alive as whatever holds it. On most platforms that is a server or your bot: if it is down, your stop is just a number. On Somnia the chain can hold it. A contract asks the chain to wake it every 5 seconds, checks the price, and sells if it is past the stop. There is no watcher to go offline, and dreamDEX perps stops already work this way.

How the chain wakes a contract

Somnia Reactivity lets a contract subscribe to events, including a system Schedule event that fires once at a chosen time. The stop contract books a wake-up 5 seconds out. Each time it wakes, it checks the price, sells if the price is past the stop, and otherwise books the next wake-up.

function onEvent(address, bytes32[] calldata, bytes calldata) external {
    require(msg.sender == REACTIVITY, "only chain");
    (bool ok, uint256 mark) = pool.tryGetMarkPrice();
    if (ok && mark <= stopPrice) { _close(mark); return; }
    _scheduleNext(); // Schedule(now + 5 s)
}
  • The sale is sent by the contract 0x09e201dAF194DdC18345bd26cb6fD23E954Cd565 itself, because Somnia delivers a reaction as a transaction from the subscription's owner. No wallet signs it.
  • The contract must hold 32 STT when it books a wake-up (a spam check, not a fee). Each wake-up is paid from its balance, about 0.003 STT a check.

dreamDEX perps already work this way

dreamDEX's own TP/SL on perps are held the same way: the exchange's stop contracts are woken by the chain every 5 seconds, and each TP or SL holds 0.15 STT to pay for its trigger. Set a stop on dreamDEX perps and the chain holds it for you (how TP/SL work on dreamDEX perps). And it is not just dreamDEX: any contract can do this, which is why I could write my own.

Fine print

  • The chain checks every 5 seconds, not continuously, so a stop can fire up to about 5 seconds after the price crosses, plus block timing. Here it was 4.8 s.
  • It reads the exchange's mark price, which is smoothed, so a very short wick may not trigger it.
  • This is testnet, with test USDso and a 0.1 ETH position, so the dollar amounts are small. What matters is where each side sold relative to the stop.

Method & reproduce

  • One throwaway testnet key opens both longs on the dreamDEX ETH perp, sent back to back at one limit price: one from its own account (the bot lane), one through the stop contract (the chain lane). Then it sets the same stop on both.
  • The bot is a plain script that polls the mark price every 2 seconds. The demo cuts it a few seconds after the stop is set and brings it back 3 minutes after the chain sells.
  • The contract, the bot, the board and every run above (runs/published/) are in the repo. Fund your own throwaway key on Somnia Tokyo testnet and run it.
